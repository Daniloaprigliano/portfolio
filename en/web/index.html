I design and develop web applications end to end, and I look after the sites of associations, magazines and cultural projects: content architecture, domain, maintenance, security. For an archive or an association a website is a working tool, not a shop window.

A dashboard that holds archives, calendar and research together

In progress

web application, continuous development

An application I designed and wrote entirely myself: it runs as a worker on serverlessinfrastructure, it has no database of its own and at every request it queries and cross-references the sources (an archive of notes and projects, mail, the calendar, a library, a press review with a voice reader), returning data computed on the fly. It includes an authenticated API through which automated agents read and write. I mention it because it is the proof of what I can build on my own: whoever teaches these tools should also have assembled them.

Websites for associations, magazines and cultural projects

Ongoing

design, development and maintenance

Among others: the cultural magazine Galápagos, which I founded, and the personal sites of writers, among them carolinacavalli.it. I follow the whole cycle (content architecture, development, domain and hosting, updates and security) with the idea that for an archive or an association a website is a working tool and not a shop window. Whoever commissions a site from me does not then have to find someone else to keep it standing.

The other sites I have built are described on the public history page, where their content belongs, and can be reached from here as well: the fifteen martyrs of Piazzale Loreto, the digital archive of «il ribelle», the Fiamme Verdi of Brescia, the Feltrinelli Foundation's digital exhibitions, the «Mai più Piazza Fontana» site, the Scuola di Cittadinanza Europea, the Risorgimento timeline and Benjamin. This site is written by hand in HTML and CSS and published on GitHub Pages.
