I teach Italian and history in a Milan upper secondary school; I hold a doctorate in Romance philology and a master’s in public history. In 2025 I ran a seven-stage course on the classroom use of artificial intelligence1 for a Milan comprehensive school; with a final-year class I reconstructed and put online the story of the fifteen martyrs of Piazzale Loreto2. Since 2013 I have kept the digital archive of «il ribelle», the clandestine paper of the Fiamme Verdi, and out of those papers came the chapter published by Marsilio3; the archive and magazine sites I have built and maintain4 are nine. Four trades doing a single job: putting people in a position to understand a text, whether it is a clandestine newspaper from 1944, an archival source or a machine’s answer. School as civic infrastructure5 is the subject I work on outside the classroom too; the full path6, with dates, is in the profile.
